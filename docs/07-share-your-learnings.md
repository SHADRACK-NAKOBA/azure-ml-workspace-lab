# Step 7 – Share your learnings

[⬅ Previous: Use Jobs to view your history](06-review-jobs.md) | [Next: Clean up resources ➡](08-cleanup.md)

A strong professional profile matters in today's job market, and sharing your labs and projects is one of the easiest ways to show you have real hands-on experience. Your LinkedIn profile is a powerful tool in your job search. If you don't have one yet, create one now.

## 7.1 Share the lab on LinkedIn

Posting your work builds your authority, increases your reach, and attracts recruiters and hiring managers.

1. Go to https://www.linkedin.com and click **Start a post**.
2. Click the **image** icon and add the screenshots you saved in step 6 (the completed job and the best model's Accuracy).
3. Write a short post in your own words. Cover:
   - **What** you built: an Azure Machine Learning workspace and an AutoML classification model that predicts diabetes.
   - **The result:** for example, *"AutoML tested 10 models and the best one, a VotingEnsemble, reached 0.95 accuracy."* Use your own numbers.
   - **What you learned:** for example, data assets and MLTable, serverless compute, how jobs track experiments.
   - **Hashtags:** `#Azure #AzureMachineLearning #AutoML #MachineLearning #DP100 #AI`
4. **Tag Prince** in the post (type `@` followed by the name and choose it from the list) so it can be shared further in the network, which increases your reach: **Prince – [LinkedIn profile](https://www.linkedin.com/)**
5. Click **Post**.

### Do's and don'ts

| ✅ Do | ❌ Don't |
|---|---|
| Show a real result (a screenshot with your metric) | Post a stock image with no proof of work |
| Explain one thing you learned in plain words | Paste the lab instructions |
| Keep it short: 5–10 lines | Share screenshots that show your subscription ID, keys or email address |

## 7.2 Share the win in the community (Progress Diary)

1. Open the Prince community and go to your **Progress Diary** thread (create one if you don't have it yet).
2. Post your screenshots with one or two lines on what you did and what you learned.

This builds your confidence on the path you're following and encourages others to do the hands-on labs too.
